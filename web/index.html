How to play

Knightshift is played on a 5×8 board. Each side starts with four knights and a royal guard on its home rank. White moves first.

Pieces

  • ♞ Knight: jumps in an L-shape like a chess knight. Captures only unprotected enemy knights.
  • ♚ Royal guard: steps one square in any direction. Protects friendly knights directly beside it (up, down, left or right). Captures unprotected knights, and can't step next to the enemy guard. It can never be captured.
  • ♜ Fortress: a knight that has been fortified. It never moves and can never be captured.

Fortify

Instead of moving, turn one of your knights on the enemy's back rank into a fortress.

Winning

  • Build two fortresses, or
  • Reduce your opponent to a single knight.
